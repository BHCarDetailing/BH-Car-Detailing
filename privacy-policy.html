Privacy Policy.

Last updated June 28, 2026. This page explains what information BH Car Detailing collects when you visit bhcardetails.com or request a quote, and how it's used.

Who We Are

BH Car Detailing ("BH," "we," "us") provides mobile car detailing, paint correction and ceramic coating services across South Florida. This policy applies to bhcardetails.com and any quote, booking or contact form on the site.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote, booking or contact form, we collect what you provide directly: your name, phone number, email address, vehicle type, requested service, general location/neighborhood, and any notes you add. We do not collect payment information through this site.

If you start our online booking form but don't finish it, we may still save your name and phone number once you've entered them, so we can follow up — this does not sign you up for text messages, which requires checking the consent box at the end of the form.

We also automatically collect basic usage data — pages visited, approximate location (city-level), device/browser type and referring site — through the analytics and advertising tools described below.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote request, answer questions, and schedule your appointment
  • To text, call or email you about your booking or service
  • To understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve it
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties except the service providers below, who process it on our behalf.

Third-Party Services We Use

This site relies on a few outside services to operate:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers form submissions to our inbox. Formspree's privacy policy
  • Calendly — powers the inline booking calendar shown after you submit a quote. Calendly's privacy policy
  • Google Analytics & Google Ads — measure site traffic and ad performance. Google's privacy policy · Google's ad settings

Each of these services may set their own cookies or collect data under their own privacy policy when their part of the page loads.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember if you've already seen or closed our first-time-client offer, so it doesn't reappear on every visit. This contains no personal information. Google Analytics, Google Ads and Calendly may separately set cookies as described in their own policies linked above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Texts & Calls

By submitting a form with your phone number, you agree that BH Car Detailing may call or text you about your quote or appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or let us know by phone or email and we'll stop contacting you.

Data Retention

We keep quote and booking information for as long as needed to provide our services and maintain business records, and delete or anonymize it when it's no longer needed.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we have about you by contacting us below. You can also opt out of Google's advertising cookies via Google Ads Settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults seeking detailing services and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as our site or services change. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest revision.

Contact Us

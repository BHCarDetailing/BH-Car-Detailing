How Add-Ons Work

Built To Stack.

Not every problem needs a full Complete Detail. Sometimes your car is otherwise clean and you just need the engine bay degreased before a sale, the dog hair pulled out of the back seat before a road trip, or a musty smell gone before you hand the keys to a valet. That's what add-ons are for — precise, single-purpose services you stack onto any wash or detail without paying for work you don't need.

Tell us what's bothering you when you book — mention it in the notes field, by text, or by phone — and we'll fold it into the same visit. Most add-ons run 30 to 60 minutes on top of your main service, so your appointment gets a little longer, not a second trip.

Exterior Add-Ons

Outside, Handled.

Engine Bay

Degrease and dress the engine bay so it looks as cared-for as the paint. Popular before a sale, trade-in or inspection. About 30 minutes.

Ceramic Spray

A spray-on ceramic sealant that adds months of hydrophobic protection and gloss on top of any wash — the quick way to extend a detail's shine. About 30 minutes.

Clay Bar Decontamination

Pulls embedded iron, tar and rail dust out of your clear coat for a glass-smooth finish — the prep step that makes wax, sealant or ceramic bond better. About 45 minutes.

Headlight Restoration

Sand and polish yellowed, hazy headlights back to clear — restores both looks and nighttime visibility. Bookable on its own or with any service. About 45 minutes.

Interior Add-Ons

Inside, Reset.

Pet Hair Removal

Heavy pet hair extraction from seats, carpet and trunk — the kind of embedded hair a normal vacuum can't lift. About 45 minutes.

Shampoo / Extraction

Hot water extraction of carpets and cloth seats — lifts stains and ground-in dirt that wiping alone won't fix. About 60 minutes.

Odor Removal

Ozone or enzyme treatment that neutralizes smoke, pet, mildew or fast-food odor at the source instead of masking it with air freshener. About 45 minutes.

Good To Know

Add-On FAQs.

Can I book an add-on without a full detail?

Most add-ons are designed to pair with a wash or detail, but Headlight Restoration can be booked entirely on its own. The rest — engine bay, pet hair, odor removal, shampoo and clay bar — are tacked onto any service at booking or requested in your message.

How much time do add-ons take?

Most run 30-60 minutes on top of your main service: engine bay and ceramic spray are about 30 minutes, pet hair and headlight restoration around 45, and shampoo/extraction runs closer to an hour depending on how much of the interior needs it.

Do you charge extra for heavy pet hair or odor?

Pricing reflects what the vehicle actually needs. Light pet hair is quick; a car that's carried a shedding dog for years takes longer and is priced accordingly — we'll always confirm before we start.

Is clay bar the same as ceramic coating?

No. Clay bar is a decontamination step that pulls embedded iron, tar and rail dust out of your clear coat, leaving it glass-smooth. Ceramic coating is a separate, longer-lasting protective layer applied after — many clients get both, in that order.

Will headlight restoration pass without new bulbs or housings?

Yes, in almost every case. Yellowed, hazy headlights are usually a UV-damaged plastic lens, not the bulb or housing. We sand and polish the lens back to clear, which restores both looks and nighttime visibility.

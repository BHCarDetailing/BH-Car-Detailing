Why We Quote These

Every Car Is Different.

Most of our menu — washes, details, even ceramic coating — fits a flat price by vehicle size because the work itself doesn't change much car to car. Specialty work isn't like that. A full PPF install on a Rolls-Royce Cullinan covers a completely different surface area than a front-end kit on a Civic. A vinyl wrap on a McLaren with deep curves takes longer than one on a flat-panel pickup. Curb rash on one wheel is a different job than curb rash on all four.

So instead of guessing with a "starting at" number that grows once we see the car, we look at it first — photos, video call, or in person — and give you one number that holds. No surprises when we show up, no upsell mid-job. That's the same standard we hold every job at BH to, just made explicit for the work where it matters most.

Paint Protection Film

PPF: Invisible Armor.

A urethane film applied to the panels most exposed to rock chips and road debris — the front bumper, hood, fenders and mirrors, or the entire car. It self-heals light scratches with heat and keeps your factory paint exactly as it left the line.

Front-End Kit

Bumper, hood, fenders and mirrors — the panels that take the brunt of highway debris. The most common install we do, usually a full day.

Full Vehicle

Every painted panel wrapped in film. Total coverage for cars that see real mileage or that you plan to keep flawless for resale.

Track & Trim Packages

Door edges, rocker panels, high-touch trim — targeted coverage for the spots that chip and scuff first, at a fraction of a full install.

Full installs are scheduled at a partner facility we coordinate — film needs a controlled, dust-free bay to bond correctly. We handle scheduling; you deal with one point of contact.

Scratch & Curb Rash Repair

Fixed, Not Hidden.

Targeted repair for the damage that bugs you every time you walk up to the car — a curbed wheel, a keyed panel, a deep scuff a normal detail can't buff out. This one's mobile: we bring the repair to you.

Good To Know

Specialty FAQs.

Why isn't specialty work priced on the site?

PPF, wraps and repair work depend entirely on the vehicle — panel count, film coverage, existing damage. We quote after seeing the car (photos or in person) so the number you get is the number you pay, not a starting-at figure that grows once we arrive.

Do you install PPF and vinyl wrap on-site?

Wraps and full PPF installs need a controlled bay — dust and temperature affect the bond — so those are scheduled at a partner facility we coordinate for you. Scratch and curb rash repair is mobile, done at your home, office or garage.

How long does PPF or a vinyl wrap take?

A full PPF install typically runs a full day to two days depending on coverage (front-end only vs. full vehicle). A full vinyl wrap usually takes a full working day, sometimes longer for complex curves or exotic bodywork.

Can you fix a curb rash on just one wheel?

Yes. Curb rash and scratch removal is billed per wheel or per affected panel, so you only pay for what's damaged — you don't have to commit to a full set.

Will PPF or ceramic coating hide existing scratches?

No — both are protective layers, not correction. If your paint already has swirls, scratches or curb rash, we handle that first (paint correction or targeted scratch repair), then apply PPF, wrap or ceramic on top of a clean surface.

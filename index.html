Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you really come to me?

Yes — BH is 100% mobile. We bring everything: water, power and professional-grade products. Your home, office or garage anywhere in South Florida becomes our studio. You don't have to drive anywhere or wait in a lobby.

How do I get a price?

Pick the package that fits your vehicle and book a time — we confirm your exact rate before we arrive. Starting packages are set for sedans, coupes and convertibles; heavily soiled vehicles, SUVs, trucks and exotics may be adjusted at booking.

How long does it take?

A wash is under an hour. A Light or Full Detail typically runs 2–4 hours depending on condition. Paint correction and ceramic coating are usually a full-day appointment so every step is done properly and given time to cure.

Do you work on exotic and luxury cars?

It's our specialty. We've cared for Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Rolls-Royce, Bentley, Porsche and AMG builds — and we give daily drivers that exact same attention to detail.
